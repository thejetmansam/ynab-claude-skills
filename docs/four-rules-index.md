# YNAB Four Rules: Quick Index

Fast reference. Full version with chapter citations in [four-rules.md](four-rules.md).

## The Question
*What do I want my money to do for me?*
(Replaces *Can I?* and *Should I?*)

## Mindset
*Forget future money; use today's money to write your future.*

## Rule Zero: Decide What Matters
Discover priorities first. Without them, the rules are busywork. In a partnership, priorities are discovered together.

## Rule One: Give Every Dollar a Job
Assign every dollar in your bank account a purpose. Priority order:
1. Immediate obligations
2. True expenses (Rule 2)
3. Highest priorities
4. Just for fun

## Rule Two: Embrace Your True Expenses
Predictable + Unpredictable-but-inevitable. Break big bills into monthly installments. Replaces emergency fund.

## Rule Three: Roll with the Punches
Changing your budget is not failing. Move money between categories as life happens. Accountable to bottom line, not line items.

## Rule Four: Age Your Money
Goal: 30+ days between earning and spending. Without it: bills waiting for money. With it: money waiting for bills.

## Spending Filter
*Does this move me closer to my goal?*

## Debt
Debt is not an option. Fund Rule 2 first, THEN attack debt. Lowest balance first. Raise income, don't just cut.

## When You Want to Quit
Six pitfalls = perfectionism. Add a $5/mo no-questions-asked fun budget. If broken, do a Budget Detox (Ch 9).

## Patterns
- **Monthly:** Budget Date (both partners)
- **Annual:** Question Everything (every line item)
- **As needed:** Fresh Start / Budget Detox
- **Always:** Credit card only spends already-budgeted dollars
- **Windfalls:** Default to next month, not this month's wants
