# Profit First: Quick Index

Fast-reference. Full text + chapter cites in [profit-first.md](profit-first.md). Michalowicz, 2014.

## The Flip
**Sales − Profit = Expenses** (not Sales − Expenses = Profit). Same math; the leverage is behavioral, take profit off the top of every deposit, run the business on what's left.

## The Four Core Principles
1. **Use Small Plates**, income disperses into separate accounts at preset %s, each with one job.
2. **Serve Sequentially**, always Profit  Owner's Pay  Tax  Expenses. If nothing's left for expenses, you can't afford those expenses (don't raid the others).
3. **Remove Temptation**, Profit + Tax at a *second bank*, no online view, no checkbook, no debit card.
4. **Enforce a Rhythm**, allocate + pay on the **10th and 25th**, never ad hoc.

## The Accounts
Core four: **Profit · Owner's Pay · Tax · Operating Expenses.** Deposits land in Operating Expenses (2017 edition adds a fifth **Income** account as the single deposit point). Profit + Tax each have a no-temptation twin at a second bank. Advanced (after 2 clean quarters): Income, The Vault, Stocking, Pass-Through, Materials, Drip, Petty Cash, Sales Tax.

## The Instant Assessment
Take-home diagnostic. **Real Revenue = Top Line − Materials & Subcontractors** (employees are OpEx, never COGS). Compare Actual % to TAP; THE BLEED = Actual − target; THE FIX = increase/decrease.

## TAP Table (Target Allocation Percentages)
| | $0-250K | $250-500K | $500K-1M | $1-5M | $5-10M | $10-50M |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Profit** | 5% | 10% | 15% | 10% | 15% | 20% |
| **Owner's Pay** | 50% | 35% | 20% | 10% | 5% | 0% |
| **Tax** | 15% | 15% | 15% | 15% | 15% | 15% |
| **OpEx** | 30% | 40% | 50% | 65% | 65% | 65% |
Tax is **15% at every band.** OpEx % is always derived last (100 − Profit − Pay − Tax).


## Start Numbers
**Historical % + 1%.** Never had profit  start Profit at 1%. New business  Profit 1 / Pay 50 / Tax 15. Ratchet ~**3 points/quarter, never backward.**

## The Rhythm (10th & 25th)
Deposits  allocate by CAPs  sweep Profit + Tax to the second bank  pay yourself a set salary  pay employees  pay bills from what's left in OpEx.

## Quarterly Distribution
First day of the quarter: take **50% of the Profit Account, leave 50%** (reserve). Split by equity. Spend the distribution **on yourself**, profit never goes back into the business ("plowback" = a lie). Pay estimated taxes same day.

## Longevity Rule of Thumb
Profit allocation if all sales stopped: **5% ≈ 3 weeks · 12% ≈ 2 months · 24% ≈ 5 months.** Reserve target = **3 months.**

## Destroying Debt
Keep taking profit while in debt. **Debt Freeze** (kill recurring charges, cut to **10% below** the assessment target, rip off the Band-Aid) + **Debt Snowball** (smallest balance first, ignore rates) + never add new debt. Distribution split while in debt: **99% to debt / 1% to you.** Negotiate: ask 25%, land 10-15%.

## Found Money (efficiency > growth)
~95% of profit is "underground" (post-sale efficiency), not more sales. Get **2× results for half the effort.** Fire the bottom client quartile (1% of revenue, −50% profit; Strategex). Selling more is the *hardest* way to raise profit.

## Sticking With It
The system's worst enemy is you. Knowing = 5%, execution = 95%. Join/run an accountability group; bring the printed Profit statement, numbers don't lie.

## Where It Falls Apart (Ch. 11)
1. **Too big too fast**  start small, +1-2%/qtr.
2. **Cutting the wrong costs**  don't cut efficiency-creating assets.
3. **"Plowback"/"reinvest"**  raiding accounts with fancy words; a card bill is debt, not expense; low OpEx = a red flag expenses are too high.
4. **Raiding the Tax Account**  estimates lag a year; earn more, owe more; check quarterly.
